Firefly Solution VS Traditional Solar

Same sun. Same energy.
One superior solution.

Traditional solar can lower your energy costs — but that's where the benefits stop. Firefly pairs solar with a battery to create a microgrid in your home or business, so you stay powered even when CFE goes down.

01

What happens when CFE goes out

The moment the grid drops, the system decides whether your home or business stays powered.

● Firefly Solution
Keeps producing and delivering power from solar and battery. Your home stays on.
○ Traditional Solar
Shuts off. Safety rules require traditional solar to stop producing when CFE goes down — even with full sun on the panels.
Why it matters This is the single biggest difference. A traditional solar system on its own can't power your home or business during a blackout.
02

Backup battery integration

Backup as a built-in feature vs. something bolted on later.

● Firefly Solution
Built in from day one. Batteries connect directly to the system, and you can add more batteries whenever your needs grow.
○ Traditional Solar
Backup isn't included. Adding it later means buying a separate battery system plus extra equipment to make everything work together — often from different brands.
Why it matters One all-in-one system means simpler wiring, one 8-year warranty, and one local team responsible for everything.
03

Protection from brownouts & power surges

Mexico's grid isn't clean — what reaches your appliances matters.

● Firefly Solution
Acts as a shield between CFE and your property. The moment it senses a voltage spike, brownout, or surge, it disconnects in a fraction of a second and delivers clean, steady power from your battery or solar. Your appliances, motors, and electronics are protected automatically.
○ Traditional Solar
Wired straight to CFE. Whatever the grid sends — spikes, dips, dirty power — flows directly to your appliances. There's no filter, no buffer, no protection.
Why it matters Voltage spikes and brownouts are common in San Miguel. Over time they shorten the life of refrigerators, AC compressors, well pumps, and electronics. Firefly absorbs those hits so your equipment doesn't.
04

Starting up your system

When can you start actually using the system you paid for?

● Firefly Solution
Turn it on the day it's installed. We set up the system so it doesn't send power back to the grid, letting you power your home or business from solar and battery right away — no waiting, no idle equipment.
○ Traditional Solar
It can't run until CFE approves you. Traditional solar pushes all its power to the grid, so nothing works until CFE installs its special two-way meter — often 3–4 months or longer. You've paid for it; it just sits there.
Why it matters CFE's two-way meter approvals can take months. A Firefly system starts saving you money from day one; a traditional solar system waits on paperwork before producing any usable energy.
