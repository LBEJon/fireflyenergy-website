Always watching

Six ways Firefly keeps your home steady.

Whatever the grid does, your home keeps its calm.

Power outage

When the grid goes dark, your home stays on — most devices won't even flicker.

Weak or unstable power

On low voltage, Firefly switches to clean, steady battery power so your fridge, AC and well pump keep running and last longer.

Poor power quality

Firefly isolates rough incoming power and delivers clean, stable power for sensitive electronics.

Sudden voltage spike

Firefly disconnects instantly, runs your home on battery and solar, then reconnects once power is safe again.

Long or planned outages

Your essentials run for hours on battery and solar — often longer, as solar refills the battery through the day.

Flicker & brief dropouts

Firefly rides through the quick sags and momentary cuts that dim your lights and reset clocks — so the everyday blips pass without a blink.
